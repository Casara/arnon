# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to fostering an inclusive, secure, and fair community for
everyone.

We support environments respecting dignity, rights, and contributions
of all people, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age,
physical traits, neurodiversity, disability, sex, gender identity,
sexual orientation, language, philosophy, religion, national origin,
socio-economic position, education level, or other status. Good-faith
participation offers equal opportunities to all.

## Encouraged Behaviors

Recognizing cultural differences, we strive to meet community
standards for positive conduct. We acknowledge that words and actions
may be received differently based on culture, background, or
language.

We commit to mindful behavior and actions reflecting shared values:

1. Respecting community purpose, activities, and gathering methods.
2. Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Taking responsibility for actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
6. Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Promoting community well-being through other constructive
   behaviors.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors violate this Code. Instances, threats, and
promotion of these constitute violations:

1. **Harassment** - violating expressed boundaries or unwanted
   personal attention after clear requests to stop.
2. **Character attacks** - insulting, demeaning comments targeting
   community members or groups.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination** - characterizing
   personality/behavior based on immutable identities.
4. **Sexualization** - inappropriate intimate behavior for the
   community context.
5. **Violating confidentiality** - sharing personal information
   without permission.
6. **Endangerment** - causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or
   harm.
7. Other behaviors threatening community well-being.

### Other Restrictions

1. **Misleading identity** - impersonating others or evading
   enforcement actions.
2. **Failing to credit sources** - not properly attributing
   contributed content.
3. **Promotional materials** - sharing commercial content outside
   community norms.
4. **Irresponsible communication** - failing to responsibly present
   restricted behavior content.

## Reporting an Issue

Not all conflicts constitute violations. When incidents occur, prompt
reporting is essential.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior
may be reported to the maintainers at
[rodrigocasara@gmail.com](mailto:rodrigocasara@gmail.com).

Moderators treat reports seriously and respond promptly. They
investigate thoroughly, review communications and records, and
interview participants while maintaining transparency and
confidentiality. Enforcement actions occur privately with involved
parties; community notification may follow mutually agreed
resolutions.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Violations trigger an enforcement ladder based on incident severity
and community impact. Ladder rungs may be skipped for serious
violations.

1. **Warning**
   - Event: single incident or incident series.
   - Consequence: private written warning.
   - Repair: private apology, responsibility acknowledgment,
     expectation clarification.
2. **Temporarily Limited Activities**
   - Event: repeated violations after warning, or first serious
     violation.
   - Consequence: written warning with a time-limited cooldown,
     possibly limiting specific channels.
   - Repair: apology, reflection during cooldown, thoughtful community
     re-entry.
3. **Temporary Suspension**
   - Event: repeated violation patterns or a single serious violation.
   - Consequence: written warning with return conditions.
   - Repair: respecting the suspension's spirit, meeting return
     conditions, thoughtful reintegration.
4. **Permanent Ban**
   - Event: repeated violations despite other measures, or an
     extremely serious violation.
   - Consequence: removal from all community spaces and channels.
   - Repair: not applicable at this severity level.

This ladder guides discretionary judgment in the community's interest.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues,
pull requests, discussions) and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the community in public spaces, including
official emails, social media accounts, or appointed event
representation.
